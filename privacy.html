Privacy Policy — Dendrites

Effective date: September 24, 2026 Contact: pixxlpickle@gmail.com

Summary

Dendrites does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Every puzzle is built into the app and the game runs entirely on your device. It goes online only when you subscribe to, restore, or check the status of Dendrites Plus through the App Store.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage data, gameplay data, or any other information. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

What stays on your device

Dendrites saves your puzzle progress on your iPhone only: the current state of each board and its undo history, your solve times, which solutions you have revealed, lessons completed, and your best gauntlet run. This information is never sent to us or anyone else, and it does not sync between devices.

Subscriptions

Dendrites Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple ID, payment details, or any other personal information. The app only asks Apple whether your subscription is active so it can unlock the extra puzzles. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the Dendrites app removes all of your saved progress from the device. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing else to delete. To cancel a subscription, use your Apple Account subscription settings; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Third parties

Dendrites contains no third-party software development kits, advertising networks, or analytics services.

Children

Dendrites is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Purchases go through Apple, and parents can require approval with Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Dendrites changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact
